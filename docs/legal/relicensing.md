## Template (English)

Subject: Licence confirmation for <show> recordings in an open wake word benchmark

Hello,

I maintain WakeWordWorld (<repo URL>), an independent, non-commercial open-source
benchmark that measures how reliably wake word engines detect names in real speech. We
build the test set only from openly licensed recordings and publish every result with
attribution.

Your recordings of <show> are ideal for this because they contain natural conversation.
On <page/feed> we found <what we found>. Could you confirm in a short reply that the
episodes are licensed under <licence>, so that we can include them with attribution
"<attribution line>"? If you prefer a different licence or would rather not be included,
that is of course fine and we will leave them out.

Thank you,
<name>, WakeWordWorld maintainer
